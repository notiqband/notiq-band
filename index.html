01 / ABOUT

WHO
WE ARE.

NOTIQ is an indie pop-rock band from Innsbruck, Austria, creating energetic and emotional music somewhere between indie, pop, rock and funk.

Somewhere between emotional songwriting, atmospheric guitars and energetic live performances, we create music that feels honest, restless and alive.

Our sound moves between indie pop, rock and funk influences — without worrying too much about fitting perfectly into one genre.

For us, music isn't about being perfect. It's about making something that feels like us.

NOTIQ is a young band based in Innsbruck, Tyrol, Austria.
